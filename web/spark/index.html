Sionna RT channel emulator on NVIDIA DGX Spark

The diagram is the source of truth. plan.md below is how it gets built; where they disagree, the diagram wins. Open the slide on its own
Description

One NVIDIA DGX Spark stands in for the radio channel between two 5G cells and two UEs. Two OCUDU DUs and two OAI UEs exchange IQ samples over ZMQ instead of over the air, and an IQ broker between them hands every block to a channel emulator running Sionna PHY on the Spark’s Blackwell GPU. For each 0.5 ms slot (30,720 samples at 61.44 Msps, 40 MHz, both cells, downlink and uplink) the emulator pulls the block, takes the current channel taps, filters the samples with them, adds noise and returns a block of the same length. The taps come from the 3GPP TDL model by default, and Sionna RT replaces them: outside the slot loop it ray-traces a 3D scene, here a UAV flying through an urban street canyon between two gNBs, and sends the path gains and delays as live taps, so the emulated channel follows the UAV. On the UE side, a UE broker joins the two UEs’ links with MPTCP.
